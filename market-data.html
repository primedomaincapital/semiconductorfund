Semiconductor Prices & Chip Market Data — Market Data

The chip industry just printed its biggest year ever and, in the same breath, priced memory like a shortage good. Both facts are true, and the tension between them is the whole story: logic and memory sales surged on AI demand while that same demand drained the DRAM supply that every other buyer depends on.

This page tracks the two series that matter most for anyone pricing chip exposure — the industry's own annual sales totals, and the memory spot and contract prices that move week to week. Annual totals tell you where the cycle stands. Memory prices tell you where it is going next.

Methodology

Included: SIA/WSTS annual and quarterly sales totals (public releases, three-month moving averages), and memory price moves as reported by Reuters, Bloomberg, and Tom's Hardware through public summaries. Excluded: paywalled TrendForce tables — no TrendForce table is reproduced verbatim here; where a summary cites TrendForce, only the summary's headline figure is used and it is attributed as such. Spot prices move weekly and are the reason this page refreshes weekly; annual SIA totals refresh when SIA publishes.

Trend labels: “Baseline (October 2026) — trend tracking starts with the next refresh” means no prior-period figure from the same source was available this session, so no movement is claimed. Where a prior figure is shown, the trend compares the current figure with that prior figure from the same source.
